# Twinnie Alpha Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026 • Invitation-only alpha

## 1. Who we are and what this Policy covers

Twinnie Inc ("Twinnie," "we," "us") is a Delaware corporation based at 1525 W Homer St, Chicago, IL 60642. This Policy explains how we handle information in the Twinnie alpha, an invitation-only preview for adults aged 18 and over located in the United States. Questions: conor@trytwinnie.com.

## 2. Information we collect

Account details: your name, phone number, and whether you opted in to Twinnie updates.

Body details: your height, weight, and gender selection.

Photos and video: a front photo, a side photo, and a short turning video that you capture or upload.

Your twin: the 3D body model we create from your photos and body details.

Try-on activity: the garments and sizes you try on.

Technical information: IP address, browser and device type, and request times, collected by our hosting and computing providers when you use the site.

Consent records: the date, time, and version of the Terms and this Policy you accepted, and your choices.

## 3. What we do not do

We do not extract, measure, or store face geometry or any other biometric identifier, and we do not use your photos to identify you. Your twin models your body shape only. Your photos may show your face because they are ordinary photos; we use them only to model your body shape.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

We do not use your photos, video, measurements, or twin to train or improve artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

We do not send you marketing texts unless you separately opted in.

We will not use your photos or twin in marketing without your separate written permission.

## 4. How we use information

We use your information to create your twin and show you garments on it; to save your account and twin so you can return to them; to rebuild or correct your twin as we improve our body-modeling technology; to contact you about your participation, and to send updates if you opted in; to understand how the alpha performs; and to keep the service secure and comply with law.

## 5. Text messages

We use your phone number to contact you about your alpha participation. If you opted in to updates, we may also send promotional texts, and you can reply STOP at any time to stop them. Opting in is never required to use the alpha. Message and data rates may apply.

## 6. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the alpha, under terms that limit their use to providing services to us:

Amazon Web Services (AWS) stores your information and runs the servers that build your twin and display the try-on view in your browser. It handles all categories of information described in Section 2.

Vercel hosts the Twinnie website. It receives technical information, and information you submit passes through it.

Google Workspace provides our email. It handles your contact details and any communications with us.

Resend sends email from our website. It handles contact details and message contents.

Some Twinnie team members and contractors work outside the United States, including in Egypt. They access tester information only when needed for their work and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, financing, or sale of the company, subject to this Policy.

## 7. Security

We encrypt information in transit and at rest, keep storage private, and limit access to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

## 8. How long we keep information

Photos, video, your twin, and try-on activity: kept while your account is active, so we can rebuild your twin as our technology improves. We delete them within 30 days after you ask us to, or if we close your account.

Account details: kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to, or if we close your account.

Consent records: kept as long as needed to show what you agreed to, even after other information is deleted.

Technical logs: kept according to our providers' standard log retention.

## 9. Your choices

Delete your information: email conor@trytwinnie.com, and we will delete your photos, video, twin, and account within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

Stop texts: reply STOP, or email us.

Access or correct: email us for a copy of your information or to correct it.

Camera: the camera turns on only after you allow it, and you can revoke permission in your browser settings.

## 10. Adults only

The alpha is for people aged 18 and over. Do not submit images of anyone else, including children. If you believe a minor's information was submitted, email us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this Policy and change the date above. For material changes, we will ask for your agreement before the new use begins.
